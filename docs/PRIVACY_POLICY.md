## 4. How We Store and Protect Your Information

### 4.1 Data Storage

**Storage Location:**
- Data is stored locally on our servers
- Financial data is stored in encrypted format
- All sensitive data is encrypted at-rest using AES-256-GCM encryption

**Data Retention:**
- Account data: Retained until account deletion
- Financial data: Retained according to our Data Retention Policy
  - Account balances: 90 days
  - Transaction history: 1 year
- Authentication logs: Retained for security purposes

### 4.2 Data Protection

**Security Measures:**
- **Encryption**: All sensitive data encrypted at-rest using AES-256-GCM
- **Authentication**: Multi-factor authentication (MFA) required
- **Access Controls**: Role-based access control (RBAC) implemented
- **Secure Transmission**: All data transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS)
- **Password Security**: Passwords hashed using bcrypt
- **Regular Security Updates**: Vulnerability management and patching procedures

**For more details, see our [Data Retention Policy](./DATA_RETENTION_POLICY.md) and [Encryption Implementation](./ENCRYPTION_IMPLEMENTATION.md).**

### 4.3 Third-Party Services

**Plaid, Inc.:**
- We use Plaid to connect your bank accounts
- Plaid processes your financial information on our behalf
- Plaid's data practices are governed by their Privacy Policy: https://plaid.com/legal/privacy-policy
- We share only the minimum information necessary for Plaid to provide their services

## 6. Your Rights and Choices

### 6.1 Access and Portability

**Right to Access:**
- You can access your personal information through your account
- You can request a copy of your data
- You can view your account information, financial data, and transaction history

**Right to Data Portability:**
- You can export your financial data
- Data is provided in a machine-readable format (JSON)

### 6.2 Correction and Updates

**Right to Rectification:**
- You can update your account information at any time
- You can correct inaccurate information
- You can modify your authentication settings

### 6.3 Deletion

**Right to Erasure:**
- You can delete your account at any time
- Account deletion removes all associated data:
  - User account information
  - Connected bank accounts
  - Account balances
  - Transaction history
- Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone

**To delete your account:**
- Use the account deletion feature in the application, or
- Contact us using the information below

### 6.4 Restriction and Objection

**Right to Restrict Processing:**
- You can request restriction of data processing
- You can object to certain types of processing

**Right to Object:**
- You can object to processing based on legitimate interests
- You can withdraw consent at any time

### 6.5 Opt-Out Rights

**Account Deletion:**
- You can delete your account at any time
- This will stop all data collection and processing

**Disconnect Bank Accounts:**
- You can disconnect bank accounts at any time
- This will remove associated financial data

### 6.6 Exercising Your Rights

To exercise any of these rights, please:
1. Use the account management features in the application, or
2. Contact us using the information provided in Section 10

We will respond to your request within 30 days.
